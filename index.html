TCS is monetising AI fastest ($2.6B annualised run-rate); Wipro is still absorbing AI-infrastructure costs. The multi-year winners among the Indian majors — Persistent, Coforge, Tech Mahindra — aren't the biggest AI books; they're mid-caps that found faster-growing niches.
