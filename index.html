Where the sector is headed

The Nifty IT index has corrected roughly 25% year-to-date in 2026, even as India's overall IT spend is forecast to top $176 billion this year (Gartner) — a valuation reset happening alongside real, if uneven, industry growth. The tension is Gen AI: sell-side analysts widely expect it to shave 2–3% a year off services revenue for the next several years, even while the same technology is expected to open an incremental $300–400 billion addressable market by 2030.

  1. FY26

    Deflationary phase begins. AI-linked productivity gains start showing up as pricing pressure on traditional application and infrastructure work.

  2. FY26–FY28

    Peak disruption. This window is expected to be the roughest stretch for revenue growth, as legacy work shrinks faster than AI-linked work scales up.

  3. FY28 (end)–FY29

    Recovery expected. AI services revenue is projected to scale enough to offset the deflation, turning the multi-year headwind into a tailwind.

The Q1 FY27 results already show the split forming. TCS is monetising AI fastest, while Wipro is still in the cost-absorption phase. Worth reading the live return numbers above with that split in mind: the multi-year winners on this board — Persistent, Coforge, Tech Mahindra — aren't the largest AI revenue books. They're mid-caps that found faster-growing niches while the giants manage a bigger, slower-moving base.
